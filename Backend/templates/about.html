About Lumora

Lumora is a smart movie recommendation application designed to help users discover movies based on their interests, preferences, and viewing activity.

What is Lumora?

Lumora brings together movie discovery and personalized recommendations in one simple platform. Users can explore movies, view detailed information, watch trailers, maintain a watchlist, and keep track of movies they have watched.

Our Recommendation System

Lumora uses movie information and user activity to provide personalized recommendations. Users can also discover movies based on different moods and languages, making it easier to find something suitable to watch.

Key Features

  • Personalized movie recommendations
  • Mood-based movie discovery
  • Multiple language options
  • Movie details and ratings
  • Trailer viewing
  • Watchlist management
  • Watch history tracking
  • Trending, new release and top-rated movies

Our Goal

The goal of Lumora is to make movie discovery easier, more personalized, and more enjoyable by helping users find movies that match their interests.
